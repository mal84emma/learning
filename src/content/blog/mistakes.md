What mistakes do we make learning while working / as adults?

- Most often we treat learning as a chore, with the mentality that hours in lead to results
  out. That's just not true — it's all a quality game.
- Why do we so rarely repeat or self-test? E.g. reading a book once. "If we really wanted
  people to learn what we have to say, every book should come with a list of key questions
  at the end — or an LLM to ask them."
- We rarely get good feedback, or even try to seek it, because it's uncomfortable. Find
  someone you trust who you don't mind telling you that you're stupid (thanks Greg).
